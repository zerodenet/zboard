# Optional observation material: bounded admission and resumable retirement

0067 adds an auxiliary retained-byte budget and per-source retirement checkpoints. New optional observation material is admitted up to 64 MiB globally and 2 MiB per source. Above these limits the financial transaction retains its source identity, hash and explicit coverage-gap receipt without the optional body. Existing excess material is preserved for controlled retirement; this is not a fixed bound on the entire database.

The shared retention task now handles expired pending/held/expired-owner material as well as applied sources. Active ownership and latest/statistics/floor references remain protected. A historical large body is verified in bounded 32-part steps before its replay authority becomes retired. A later transaction rechecks each part's digest before deleting it and decrements the retained-byte authority atomically. Durable verification/deletion checkpoints survive interruption. Corruption is quarantined with a review delay; another healthy source can proceed. Retired material cannot be revived through the administrator retry entry.

The business fact and observation admission remain one transaction. Batch admission locks immutable source headers before the shared byte authority, preserving a common order across overlapping batches. Retirement and retry use node/state/header authority order. Missing recorded budget/checkpoint tables or the singleton refuse startup/copy rather than seeding zero. SQLite's unrestricted field types are guarded by byte metadata before material reads; MySQL receives explicit formal DDL and reconciliation checks. No released migration is edited.

The administrator queue exposes retired state, the coverage gap, original material size and remaining cleanup bytes. An omitted observation is unknown; it is not an applied configuration, zero load, revoked entitlement or financial failure. Financial/report receipts, settlement cursors, immutable headers and audit history remain retained and continue to grow with accepted facts. This batch therefore bounds optional raw material, not all authoritative storage. Long-term archival of authoritative business history remains a separate, unverified operational boundary.

Rollback preserves the new auxiliary authority and retired source receipts; the down migration deliberately refuses destructive removal. Use an intact pre-upgrade backup for a version rollback, including all financial and observation authority, and reconcile any facts accepted afterward. Do not run an older binary against a database containing retired material or remove its evidence to make it open.
